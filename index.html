About NIDRA-AI

NIDRA-AI is an educational website prototype exploring how AI-assisted information may support fertility education and informed clinical conversations.

Important medical notice

This prototype does not diagnose conditions, calculate personal miscarriage risk, or prevent pregnancy loss. IVF outcomes depend on many factors, and not all pregnancy losses can be prevented. Always consult a qualified fertility specialist about personal medical decisions.

Questions to discuss with your specialist

  • What factors are relevant to my individual treatment?
  • What is the purpose of each prescribed medication?
  • Which symptoms mean I should contact the clinic?
  • What follow-up appointments or tests should I expect?
